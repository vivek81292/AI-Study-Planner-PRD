# Product Requirements Document

## Product Name

AI Study Planner

## Objective

Help students organize their study schedules efficiently.

## Problem Statement

Students spend significant time deciding what to study and when.

This often results in:
- Missed deadlines
- Poor exam preparation
- Increased stress

## Solution

Generate personalized study plans using AI.

The user enters:

- Subjects
- Exam dates
- Available hours

The system generates a daily study plan.

## MVP Features

### Feature 1
Subject Input

### Feature 2
Exam Date Tracking

### Feature 3
AI Schedule Generator

### Feature 4
Daily Progress Tracking

## Out of Scope

- Live tutoring
- Video lessons
- Group study rooms

## Success Criteria

- 40% weekly retention
- 70% study plan completion
