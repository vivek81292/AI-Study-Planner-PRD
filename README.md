# AI Study Planner

A product management case study for an AI-powered study planning application.

## Problem

Students often struggle to create realistic study schedules before exams.

Many students:
- Procrastinate
- Underestimate workload
- Miss deadlines

## Proposed Solution

An AI-powered study planner that automatically generates personalized study schedules based on:

- Subjects
- Exam dates
- Available study hours
- Difficulty level

## Target Users

- High school students
- College students
- Competitive exam aspirants

## Success Metrics

### North Star Metric
Weekly Study Sessions Completed

### Supporting Metrics
- Daily Active Users
- Retention Rate
- Schedule Completion Rate
